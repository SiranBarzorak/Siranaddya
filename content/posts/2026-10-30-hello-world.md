Every personal site starts with a hello-world post. This is mine.

I wanted somewhere to write that I fully own: plain HTML files, no tracking, no platform that might disappear or change its rules. So this site is a set of simple pages that I write in plain text.

## What to expect here

- Notes on learning design and what makes training stick
- Lessons from rolling out new tools and ways of working
- Short posts on talent development, skills, and change adoption
