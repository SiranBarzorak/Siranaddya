Every personal site starts with a hello-world post. This is mine.

I wanted somewhere to write that I fully own: plain HTML files, no tracking, no platform that might disappear or change its rules. So this site is built by a small script that turns Markdown files into static pages.

## What to expect here

- Notes on projects I'm working on
- Write-ups of bugs that took far too long to find
- Short posts on tools and ideas I find useful

If you'd like to follow along, there's an [RSS feed](../feed.xml).
